# Ebooks gratuitos — Semana 25

Libros completos que sus autores publican gratis en la web, en inglés.

| Libro | Para qué |
|---|---|
| [Python for Data Analysis, 3rd edition](https://wesmckinney.com/book/) (Wes McKinney) | Del creador de pandas. El capítulo 8, ["Data Wrangling: Join, Combine, and Reshape"](https://wesmckinney.com/book/data-wrangling.html), cubre las teorías 02 y 03; el 10, ["Data Aggregation and Group Operations"](https://wesmckinney.com/book/data-aggregation.html), la 01; el 11, ["Time Series"](https://wesmckinney.com/book/time-series.html), la 04. Escrito para pandas 1.4: algunos textos de `freq` (`"H"`, `"M"`) han cambiado |
| [Python Data Science Handbook](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/) (Jake VanderPlas) | Del capítulo 3: ["Combining Datasets: Merge and Join"](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/03.07-merge-and-join.html), ["Aggregation and Grouping"](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/03.08-aggregation-and-grouping.html) y ["Working with Time Series"](https://jakevdp.github.io/PythonDataScienceHandbook/03.11-working-with-time-series.html). De 2016: lee los avisos de la versión |
| [Modern pandas](https://tomaugspurger.net/posts/modern-1-intro/) (Tom Augspurger) | Dos artículos de la serie para esta semana: ["Reshaping & Tidy Data"](https://tomaugspurger.net/posts/modern-5-tidy/) y ["Timeseries"](https://tomaugspurger.net/posts/modern-7-timeseries/), con datos reales |
